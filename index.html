TradePro — Institutional Trading & AI Wealth Management Platform

TradePro is an institutional-grade trading workstation and AI wealth management platform providing real-time multi-market data, quantitative risk analytics, simulated paper execution, and intelligent voice interaction.

What is TradePro and Who is it For?

TradePro is an institutional-grade financial analytics and simulated execution platform engineered and operated by Aditya Paikaray. TradePro solves the fragmentation of retail trading tools by combining live multi-exchange market telemetry (US and Indian Equities), quantitative portfolio risk attribution (Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio, max drawdown), and Google Gemini-powered conversational intelligence into a single low-latency workstation.

TradePro is designed for:

  • Active Retail Traders: Traders seeking real-time candlestick charts, simulated order routing, and rapid keyboard or voice execution.
  • Quantitative Investors: Researchers seeking deterministic portfolio risk metrics, sector concentration caps, and downside volatility modeling.
  • AI Finance Observers: Analysts exploring LLM-assisted scenario stress tests, macroeconomic comparisons, and natural language portfolio querying.

Main Workstation Features

  • AI Wealth Command Center: Multi-modal financial co-pilot for stress testing, allocation shifts, and scenario simulations.
  • Native Voice Assistant: Hands-free voice commands to query stock quotes, switch views, and trigger simulated orders.
  • Interactive Candlestick Charts: Real-time TradingView Lightweight Charts with volume overlays, moving averages, and multiple timeframes.
  • Real-Time Index Heatmap: Sector and capitalization heatmaps visualizing market breadth and percentage movements across NIFTY 50 and S&P 500.
  • Quantitative Portfolio Analytics: Mathematical equity curve modeling, Sharpe ratio calculation, win/loss trade splits, and maximum drawdown tracking.
  • Dual-Tab Secure Authentication: Secure 6-digit Email OTP authentication with cryptographic rate limiting and password fallback.

How TradePro Works: 5-Stage Architecture

  1. Telemetry Ingestion: Continuous quote feeds from US (NYSE, NASDAQ) and Indian (NSE, BSE) exchanges normalized into a sub-second analytical pipeline.
  2. Quantitative Modeling: Automated risk engine calculating portfolio volatility, Sharpe ratios, and drawdowns deterministically.
  3. AI Wealth Co-Pilot: Gemini models analyzing portfolio holdings and historical stress scenarios with zero mathematical hallucination.
  4. Voice Processing: Local browser voice transcription routed to safety parsers for hands-free navigation.
  5. Simulated Execution: Risk-managed simulated paper ledger with realistic bid-ask spread and slippage dynamics.

Supported Markets & Instruments

TradePro supports real-time market data across US Equities (NYSE, NASDAQ), Indian Equities (NSE, BSE), global benchmark indices (S&P 500, NASDAQ 100, NIFTY 50, SENSEX), commodities, and major foreign exchange currency pairs.
